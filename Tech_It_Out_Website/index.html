FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Tech It Out support free?

Basic everyday technology guidance, such as Wi-Fi troubleshooting and product-choice guidance, is offered free. Paid work may apply to repairs, advanced support, products or other services.

Do you have prices yet?

Pricing is being finalised. The website is designed so prices can be added or changed later from one central configuration file.

Can I request a repair online?

Yes. Use the repair/support form and provide the device and problem. Your request is sent to the Tech It Out system for review.

Can I pay online?

Yes, once your Stripe account and live service prices are configured. The supplied project includes the payment-ready structure without exposing payment secrets in the website.

Can I add more photos later?

Yes. Place additional images in the images folder and add them to the gallery configuration when you are ready.
